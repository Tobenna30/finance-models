## Remaining limitations

1. Final $1,735m/$410m debt, spreads, OID and tenors rely on the existing secondary financing sources. Obtain final executed documents before presenting them as independently confirmed.
2. Precedent headline values mix equity and enterprise values. The 879p median remains an indicative cross-check, not a clean EV valuation. Do not silently treat KnowBe4 equity value as enterprise value.
3. Deferred revenue, tax EBITDA, interest restriction and carryforwards are simplified. No tax advice or covenant compliance conclusion follows from this model.
4. No revolver, intra-year liquidity, minimum-cash funding, additional funding fees/interest or debt recovery waterfall is modelled. Existing IRRs therefore exclude funding needed to restore the cash minimum.
5. The model combines dated reported and assumed inputs. FY24 revenue uses the July update; the 23% FY24 margin and December cash proxy remain assumptions. No new source was used to replace them in this pass.
6. The stated cash minimum is a model assumption, not a verified covenant. A shortfall does not itself prove a contractual default.
