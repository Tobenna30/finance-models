# Vitamin Well / Vital Bidco: reject pending downside protection
*Project 09. Public-information underwriting, 27 October 2026. SEKm unless stated. Full-year pro forma scenarios, not audited forecasts or contractual covenant tests.*

## Decision
Reject an unconditional commitment at the modelled SEK20,900m term-loan equivalent. Base entry capacity is SEK21,422m under assumed 5.5x leverage and 2.0x interest-cover limits, leaving only 2.4% EBITDA headroom. Downside leverage peaks at 7.52x and cover falls to 1.44x. Liquidity remains available, but it does not repair the earnings risk.

This is a request for stronger protection and evidence, not a claim the borrower has breached its loan agreement. Re-underwrite with verified debt, cash, addbacks, pricing, security, ranking and covenant definitions before approval.

## Borrower and transaction
Cinven's July announcement identifies EMPWR as a manufacturing partner being acquired by Vitamin Well. Transaction consideration was undisclosed and approvals were pending. That brings capacity and integration risk into a brand-led borrower. S&P's September rating action reports EUR1.9bn-equivalent term debt due in 2033 and a EUR200m revolver for refinancing and the planned acquisition. The published issuer rating is B+; new pricing is unverified. Use the newer structure; a legacy lender's disclosed S+450bp loan is not evidence of the new loan's pricing.

## Cash underwriting
Translate both facilities at an assumed 11.0 SEK/EUR. Starting adjusted EBITDA is the midpoint of the agency's SEK4.0-4.2bn forecast, not an audited result. Revenue is inferred from a 26% margin proxy. Deduct a 5% cash-EBITDA reserve, tax, capex, working capital and one-off transaction costs. Base loan interest is assumed at 7%; downside adds 200bp. Forecasts include RCF fees, draws, repayments and a cash sweep, without a funding plug.

## Capacity and recovery
Capacity is the lower of cash EBITDA times the assumed leverage ceiling and debt supported at the assumed interest-cover floor, after commitment fees. At entry the leverage limit binds. This is a gross funded-debt screen; it excludes unverified leases, other borrowings and rating-agency adjustments. It is not maximum safe acquisition leverage.

Default recovery is 58.8%. Apply a 35% haircut to starting cash EBITDA, 6x enterprise value, 10% enforcement costs and SEK100m priority claims. Fully draw the revolver and share the remaining pool pari passu with the term loan. First-lien claims total SEK23,100m; the available pool is SEK13,571m. Legal priorities, security perimeter and the multiple are unverified. The workbook varies haircuts and multiples; this is a stress, not a prediction or a reproduction of a rating recovery estimate.

## Monitoring and unresolved evidence
Quarterly: reconcile reported cash EBITDA and addbacks; test actual covenant definitions; track liquidity and RCF availability; monitor capex commitments, customer demand and integration. The model's 5.5x and 2.0x limits trigger underwriting review only. Forecast output is a template for monitoring, not a record of actual monitoring meetings or borrower reporting.

There is no audited group balance sheet or disclosed purchase-price bridge in this model. Opening cash, FX, rates, fees and amortisation are assumptions. Cash and debt rollforwards reconcile separately. New-draw interest begins next period, so the annual convention understates interest when facilities are drawn during a year. FX is held constant and the currency mix is not modelled.

The model's SEK-99m 2026 FCF is less negative than the agency's approximately SEK700-800m outflow forecast. Public data do not provide a complete reconciliation. Do not calibrate missing costs to close that gap or present the model as a reproduction of agency FOCF. The refinancing/EMPWR perimeter is pro forma; this is not a remaining-2026 cash forecast from today's date.
